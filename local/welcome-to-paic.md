# Welcome to PAIC

Platteville AI Club is UW-Platteville's student chapter of the [ALL Applied AI Network](https://all-ai-network.org) — a nationwide community of university AI clubs.

## What we do

PAIC runs its own events and projects on campus. As an ALL chapter, we also get access to shared resources:

- **Weekly events** — workshops, speaker nights, and project time. Show up, learn by doing.
- **Real projects** — hackathons, Innovation Labs, and semester-long builds that go on your portfolio.
- **Shared curriculum** — a learning tree that starts at absolute zero and scales to shipping production AI apps.
- **Recognition** — check in at events, earn badges, and build a verified record on the ALL network.

## Who it's for

Every major. Every experience level. If you want to build with AI — or aren't sure where to start — PAIC is for you.
